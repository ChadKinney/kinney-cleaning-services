Airbnb Turnover Cleaning in Mobile, AL: What Guests Notice First

November 9, 2026

Short term rental guests decide fast. They walk in, look around, and form an opinion in the first minute. A turnover clean is the reset between one guest leaving and the next one arriving, and it has to be right every single time. There is no second chance with a guest who finds a hair in the shower or a sticky counter. Here is what a professional turnover covers for hosts in Fairhope, Daphne, Spanish Fort, and across Mobile.

What a turnover clean covers

  • Fresh linens and beds made hotel style. Sheets, pillowcases, and towels swapped for clean sets, beds made tight, and throws folded the same way every time.
  • Bathrooms reset to spotless. Showers, tubs, toilets, sinks, and mirrors cleaned and dried, with fresh towels, toilet paper, and soap in place.
  • Kitchen reset. Counters wiped, sink cleaned, dishes checked, coffee maker emptied and wiped, and the fridge checked for anything left behind.
  • Floors throughout. Vacuumed and mopped, including under beds and behind doors where dust and lost items collect.
  • Trash out, surfaces wiped. Every trash can emptied and relined, every table, nightstand, and remote wiped down.
  • The front door first impression. Porch swept, entry mat shaken out, and the door and handle wiped, since that is the first thing a guest touches.

The timing window matters

Most turnovers run on a tight window, often check out at 11 and check in at 3 or 4. That leaves a few hours to reset a whole home. A regular turnover crew learns your place and gets faster each visit, because the layout, the linen closet, and the supply spots stay the same. If your bookings stack up on weekends, lock in a recurring slot so your turnovers are covered before the calendar fills.

What guests notice first

  • Smell. A fresh, neutral smell says clean. Lingering cooking smells or dampness say the opposite. Ventilate while cleaning and check the AC filter.
  • Bathrooms. Guests judge the whole stay by the bathroom. Water spots on the mirror or hair in the drain will show up in a review.
  • Beds. Crisp sheets and a neatly made bed set the tone. A wrinkled or stained sheet is the fastest way to lose a star.
  • Floors under bare feet. Guests walk barefoot. Grit on a hard floor or sand tracked in from the coast gets noticed right away.

Supplies and restocking

A turnover is not just cleaning. It is restocking too. Keep a simple checklist on the inside of a cabinet door: toilet paper, paper towels, soap, shampoo, trash bags, coffee, and clean linens. A crew that knows your checklist restocks the same way every time, so guests never find an empty soap dish. Hosts who keep a locked owner closet with backup supplies make the crew's job easier and the guest experience steadier.

How pricing works

Turnover pricing depends on the size of the rental, the number of beds and baths, and how often you book. A one bedroom condo in Fairhope and a four bedroom house on the Eastern Shore are different jobs. Every turnover plan gets a custom quote, and recurring hosts get a steady rate they can plan around. Call or use the quote form with your bedroom and bathroom count, your check out and check in times, and how many turnovers you run in a typical month.
