Selling Soon in Mobile, AL: Exterior Cleanup Before You List

October 26, 2026

Buyers make up their minds fast. In Mobile, Daphne, Spanish Fort, and Fairhope, the first photos online and the first look from the driveway tell a buyer whether a home feels cared for or neglected. Algae on siding, a stained driveway, and dirty windows do not mean a home is in bad shape, but they read that way in photos. Exterior cleanup is one of the cheapest ways to change that first impression before you list.

The order matters. Work from the top down and from the street inward, so each job does not dirty the one you just finished.

1. Roof and siding first

Start with what shows in the widest photos. A roof soft wash is $549 for one story homes up to 2,000 sq ft of roof, and a house soft wash runs $349 for a 1,000 sq ft home up to $899 for a 4,000 sq ft home, with two story homes doubling. Together they reset the whole look of the house. A buyer sees clean siding and a streak free roof before they notice anything else, and the listing photos get noticeably brighter.

If the budget only covers one, pick the siding wash. It covers more visible surface, and it is what a buyer sees at eye level from the yard.

2. Gutters and downspouts

Gutter cleaning is $179 for one story homes. Overflowing or stained gutters worry buyers because they hint at water problems, even when the real issue is just leaves. Clean gutters, clear downspouts, and no streaks down the fascia tell a buyer the home has been looked after. Book gutters right after the roof wash, since roof debris lands in the gutters.

3. Driveway, walkways, and patio

The driveway is the path a buyer walks to the front door. Short driveways wash for $149, long driveways for $199, and extra long driveways for $279. A concrete patio and walkway wash starts at $200 for 400 sq ft. In West Mobile and Tillmans Corner, where driveways collect pine needles and red dirt, this one job alone can change how the whole front yard photographs.

Do the driveway after the house wash, not before, so rinse water from the siding does not leave fresh streaks on clean concrete.

4. Windows last

Windows go last because every other wash can splash them. Window cleaning is $129 for up to 10 windows and $199 for 11 to 20 windows on one story homes, inside and out. Clean windows let light into the home for interior photos too, which is a bonus most sellers forget. A bright interior photo set starts with glass that is actually clear.

5. Inside touches that pair well

If the home has tile floors, a tile and grout cleaning is $149 for 1 room, $269 for 2 rooms, and $379 for 3 rooms. Entry tile and kitchen tile matter most for showings, since buyers walk on them first and stand on them while they talk about the home. Fresh grout lines make tile read as newer without any remodel cost.

Timing it before photos

  • Book 1 to 2 weeks before photos. That leaves room for weather delays and for the yard to be tidied after the washes.
  • Do washes before mulch and flowers. Wash water and foot traffic can disturb fresh beds.
  • Check the forecast. A wash the day before a heavy rain still helps, but a dry day before photos is ideal.
  • Bundle when you can. Booking 3 or more services gets 10 percent off on the pay page, which makes the full curb package easier to justify.

None of this replaces repairs a home truly needs. But for a home that is already in good shape, exterior cleanup removes the distractions that make buyers hesitate, and it does it in a day or two instead of a remodel timeline.
