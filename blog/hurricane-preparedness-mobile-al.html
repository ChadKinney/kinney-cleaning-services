Hurricane Preparedness Checklist for Mobile Homeowners

October 7, 2026

Tropical Storm Isaias is forecast to strengthen into a hurricane, possibly a high end Category 2 with winds near 110 mph, bringing torrential rain, storm surge, and possible tornadoes to our area late Friday into Saturday. Today and tomorrow are your prep window. Here is the checklist we give our own families and customers, split into outdoor and indoor prep.

Outside your home

  • Bring it in or tie it down. Patio furniture, grills, potted plants, kids toys, trash cans, garden tools. Anything loose becomes a projectile in 75 mph gusts.
  • Clear your gutters and downspouts. Clogged gutters plus torrential rain means water pouring where you do not want it. If yours are packed, this is the single most useful thing you can do today.
  • Clear storm drains near your home so street water can drain instead of backing up.
  • Trim dead branches hanging over your house or driveway, if you can do it safely today.
  • Secure gates and check fence sections that are already weak.
  • Move vehicles away from large trees and to higher ground if your street floods.
  • Do not drain your pool. Turn off the pump and secure or store loose pool equipment.
  • Take photos of your home's exterior now. If there is damage later, before photos make insurance claims much smoother.

Inside your home

  • Water and food. One gallon of water per person per day for at least three days, plus nonperishable food and a manual can opener.
  • Charge everything. Phones, power banks, flashlights. Fresh batteries.
  • Fill bathtubs and sinks with water for flushing and basic washing.
  • Medications and first aid. A week of prescriptions if you can, plus a basic first aid kit.
  • Important documents in a waterproof bag. Snap photos of them on your phone too.
  • Turn your fridge and freezer to the coldest setting. Freeze water bottles to help keep things cold if power goes out.
  • Unplug sensitive electronics or use surge protectors.
  • Cash on hand. If power is out, card readers are down.
  • Gas up vehicles and your generator now. Never run a generator inside the house or garage.
  • Pet supplies. Food, water, carriers, leashes, medications.
  • Know your evacuation zone and your route out. If officials say go, go.
  • Stay away from windows during the worst of it if you do not have storm shutters. Close interior doors to compartmentalize.

After the storm passes

  • Wait for the all clear. Do not touch downed power lines.
  • Photograph all damage before you start cleanup.
  • Clear debris from drains and gutters so the next rain can drain.
  • Be careful with chainsaws and ladders. Storm cleanup injuries are common.

Track the storm

Forecasts change fast. Check these before you make plans:

  • National Hurricane Center, official advisories, forecast track, and watches and warnings for Tropical Storm Isaias.
  • FOX Weather: Mobile, AL — local forecast and radar for the Alabama Gulf Coast.
  • The Weather Channel — hourly forecasts and storm coverage.
