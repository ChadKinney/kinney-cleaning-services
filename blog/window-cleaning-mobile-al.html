Professional Window Cleaning in Mobile, AL: What to Expect

October 19, 2026

Windows in Mobile take a beating. Pine pollen coats them yellow every spring, salt air hazes them on the Eastern Shore, sprinklers leave hard water spots, and our humidity grows mildew in the tracks. Clean glass changes how your whole house feels from the inside out, and it is one of the cheapest ways to brighten a home before the holidays.

What a professional window cleaning includes

  • Interior and exterior glass. Both sides, squeegeed and detailed, so there is no haze left behind.
  • Screens washed. Dirty screens re dirty the glass the first time it rains. We wash them so the clean lasts.
  • Sills and tracks wiped out. Dead bugs, dirt, and mildew get cleared from the tracks so your windows open smooth again.
  • Hard water spot treatment. Sprinkler spots and mineral buildup get extra attention where they are etched in.
  • Final walkaround. We check every pane from the inside in daylight, which is where streaks show up.

How often Mobile homes need it

Most homes do well with a professional cleaning twice a year: once after pollen season in spring, and once in the fall before the holidays. Homes near the water in Daphne, Spanish Fort, or Fairhope, and homes with big pine trees over the roof in Saraland or Satsuma, may want a third visit in summer. If you cannot remember the last time yours were cleaned, you are overdue.

What it costs

Our window cleaning is priced flat by the number of windows. One story homes run $129 for up to 10 windows and $199 for 11 to 20. Two story homes run $179 for up to 10 and $269 for 11 to 20. Screens, sills, and tracks are included. Count your windows, pick your tier, and that is the price.

Do it right and pair it with the porch

Windows and porches go together. The same pollen and mildew that film your glass coat the porch floor, the columns, and the front door. A porch wash with your window cleaning makes the whole front of the house look new. Ask when you book and we will price both as one job.

Why homeowners keep booking us

We show up on time, we treat your home like our own, and we stand behind the work. That is how a veteran owned local business earns its 5-Star Google Reviews, one clean window at a time.
