# Lead swap pitch kit

Ready to copy and send. Pick one trade, text or email three to five companies, keep it to one partner per trade.

## Text message version

Hey [Name], this is Chad with Kinney Cleaning Services here in Mobile. My cleaning jobs put me in front of local homeowners every week, and a lot of them ask me about roof work, lawns, and pest control while I am there. I am looking to trade referrals with one solid [roofer / landscaper / pest control company] in the area. You send me the houses that need a wash, I send you the ones that need your work. No fees, just real customers both ways. Open to a quick chat about it?

## Email version

Subject: Quick idea for trading leads in Mobile

Hi [Name],

I run Kinney Cleaning Services, a veteran owned cleaning company here in Mobile. My crews are in front of local homeowners every week, and they ask me all the time about roof work, lawn care, pest control, and gutters while we are on the job.

I am putting together a small referral circle with one trusted company per trade. The deal is simple. When a customer asks me about your kind of work, I send them straight to you. When your crew sees a house that needs a wash, you send them to me. No fees and no middleman, just real local customers flowing both ways.

My business is rated 5.0 on Google with all 5 star reviews, and I only want to partner with companies I would hire myself.

Open to a 10 minute call this week to see if it fits? You can reach me at (251) 767-0754.

Chad Kinney
Kinney Cleaning Services
kinneycleaningservices.com
